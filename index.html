Day 01 / Saturday · Melbourne

Arrive in Melbourne

Sleep · a family home
Morning
Singapore → Melbourne

Aim for a nonstop flight landing by midday. Head into Melbourne and drop bags at a family home.

13:00
The first Melbourne walk

Start at Flinders Street Station and Federation Square, then head through Degraves Street and the CBD laneways for coffee and lunch.

15:30
Royal Botanic Gardens

Take a relaxed garden loop before a group dinner. Garden details ↗

City lights

Tonight

Five adults: drinks and dancing at ATTIK ↗; take a taxi or rideshare home.

Under-18 plan: alcohol-free movie or games at the house with the host or another agreed trusted adult.

Day 02 / Sunday · Melbourne

Market, zoo and beaches

Sleep · a family home
09:00
Queen Victoria Market

Breakfast, coffee and a stroll through the stalls. Sunday trading is 9am–4pm. Market hours ↗

11:15
Melbourne Zoo

Allow about three hours for the zoo and lunch. It is currently open 9am–5pm. Zoo details ↗

15:00
Brighton → St Kilda

See Brighton’s bathing boxes, then walk St Kilda’s foreshore and pier. In ski season, treat these as scenic winter beach stops.

Beach day

Tonight

Five adults: Sunday club night at StoryVille ↗. Keep it early and have the next morning’s driver stay sober and get enough sleep.

Under-18 plan: joins dinner, then returns to the house for an all-ages evening with the agreed adult.

Day 03 / Monday · Mt Buller

First day at Mount Buller

Sleep · Mount Buller
07:00
Drive from Melbourne

Have the rental ready before departure. Allow around three hours before stops and resort entry. The driver should be rested and sober. Mt Buller FAQ ↗

11:00
Gear up

Collect prebooked skis or boards, boots and helmets; first-timers take a lesson. Leave luggage at your accommodation if it offers storage.

Afternoon
Ski and snowboard

Ride through the afternoon, then collect your luggage and check in for one night on the mountain.

Snow day one

Tonight

Mountain dinner with all six. Keep it relaxed so Tuesday’s ride and drive feel good.

Day 04 / Tuesday · Mt Buller → Colac

Morning on the snow, then Colac

Sleep · a family home
08:30
Breakfast and checkout

Check whether the lodge can hold your bags while you ride. Pack the car before heading to the slopes if needed.

09:00
Second snow session

Ski or snowboard through the morning, then return rental gear and eat lunch. This is a shorter second day on the mountain.

13:30
Direct drive to Colac

Budget around 6–7 hours with breaks and traffic. Two rested drivers make the late arrival easier; no one drinks before their driving is finished.

Snow day two

Tonight

Late dinner and a home party at a family home. Adults can have drinks after arrival; the under-18 traveller has alcohol-free options.

Day 05 / Wednesday · Colac

A day at home in Colac

Sleep · a family home
Late AM
Sleep in

Take the morning slowly after two snow days and the drive across Victoria.

All day
Stay home and chill

Cook, order food, play games, talk, rest and spend time with the hosts. Leave the whole day free.

Evening
Home party

Music, food and drinks for the adults; mocktails and other alcohol-free drinks for the under-18 traveller.

Colac pause

Tonight

No bookings, no rushing. Tomorrow’s driver should finish the night with enough rest for the return trip.

Day 06 / Thursday · Colac → Melbourne

Back to Melbourne

Sleep · a family home
Morning
Breakfast with the hosts

Pack, thank the hosts and drive back toward Melbourne. Allow roughly 2½–3 hours with a break and traffic.

Afternoon
Free city time

Wander Fitzroy’s shops and street art, or revisit your favourite CBD lanes. Drop bags at a family home.

19:00
Group dinner

Have dinner all together before the adults’ final Melbourne club night. Pack for the Sydney flight.

Final city night

Tonight

Five adults: New Guernica’s Thursday night ↗.

Under-18 plan: an alcohol-free night at a family home with the agreed adult.

Day 07 / Friday · Melbourne → Sydney

Sydney Harbour

Sleep · Sydney, night 1 of 3
Morning
Return the car and fly MEL → SYD

Aim for a late-morning flight from Melbourne Airport. Allow time for the rental return and domestic bag drop. The flight takes about 1 hour 25 minutes.

Early PM
Drop bags near the CBD

Use the airport train or share a ride. A base near the CBD or Circular Quay keeps the harbour and ferry easy to reach.

15:00
Circular Quay, the Opera House and The Rocks

Walk to the Opera House forecourt, then follow the waterfront to The Rocks for Harbour Bridge views.

Tonight

Dinner with all six in The Rocks or the CBD.

Day 08 / Saturday · Sydney coast

Bondi and Tamarama

Sleep · Sydney, night 2 of 3
09:30
Bondi Beach and brunch

Head to Bondi for coffee, the promenade and time on the sand.

11:30
Coastal walk to Tamarama

Follow the open sections towards Tamarama. Turn back there for a manageable winter walk and check local closures before leaving.

Afternoon
Return to the city

Leave the rest of the afternoon open. If coastal weather turns, swap the walk for the Art Gallery of New South Wales.

Tonight

Group dinner in Chinatown or Surry Hills.

Day 09 / Sunday · Sydney Harbour → Manly

Manly by ferry

Sleep · Sydney, night 3 of 3
10:00
Circular Quay → Manly

Take the Manly ferry for views of the Opera House and bridge from the water.

Late AM
Manly Beach and Shelly Beach

Walk down the Corso to Manly Beach, then follow the foreshore to Shelly Beach. Stop for fish and chips or a café lunch.

Afternoon
Ferry back to Circular Quay

Return with time for a waterfront wander. Check the ferry timetable once dates are set.

Tonight

Final dinner for all six. Pack for the Monday flight.

Driving and evenings

The longest drive is Mount Buller → Colac. Allow about 6–7 hours with breaks, and have a rested, sober driver. Everyone joins the group dinners. The three Melbourne club nights are adults only. The under-18 traveller has alcohol-free drinks at the Colac home parties.
